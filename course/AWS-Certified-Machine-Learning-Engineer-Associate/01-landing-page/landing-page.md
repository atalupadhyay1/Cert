## ❓ Frequently asked questions

**Is this the real exam?**
No. These are original practice questions written for this course. Nothing is copied from or recalled from a live exam, and no question bank was used as a source.

**Which version of the exam does this target?**
MLA-C02, the updated exam that replaced MLA-C01 in English on 29 September 2026. The domains, tasks and skills in every explanation come from the MLA-C02 exam guide.

**How many questions are on the real exam?**
The published form is **65 questions in 130 minutes**, of which **50 are scored and 15 are unscored**; AWS does not identify the unscored items. Each set here is 65 items. While MLA-C02 is in beta it is delivered as 85 questions in 170 minutes with the extra items unscored.

**What is the passing score?**
AWS publishes a **minimum passing score of 720 on a scaled range of 100–1000**, with compensatory scoring, so there is no separate pass mark per domain. Because 720 is scaled rather than a raw percentage, this course does not convert practice results into an expected exam score. Beta sittings do not carry a pass/fail result.

**Why are there no case studies?**
Because the published MLA-C02 format has none. The exam is multiple choice and multiple response only, so no case-study scaffolding is included or imitated.

**How many multiple-response items are on the real exam?**
AWS does not publish a split between multiple choice and multiple response. Each set here carries **50 multiple-choice and 15 multiple-response** items; that mix is an authoring choice and is labelled as one everywhere it appears.

**Does this cover generative AI, or only classic machine learning?**
Both. The MLA-C02 guide covers SageMaker AI for traditional models and Amazon Bedrock for foundation models, including RAG, fine-tuning, agents, prompt management, GenAI evaluation and guardrails, and the bank follows that scope.

**Do I need hands-on experience?**
AWS recommends at least one year of experience using SageMaker AI, Amazon Bedrock and other ML engineering AWS services, plus a year in a related role. No prerequisite is enforced.

**How should I use the six exams?**
Take Exam 1 untimed for a baseline, study the domains where you lost most items, work through Exams 2–5 with a light clock, and finish with Exam 6 under the full 130 minutes.

**Does a good score guarantee I will pass?**
No. Practice performance is not a scaled score, and no practice result predicts the outcome of a live appointment.

**Practice percentages are not scaled scores.** A score of 80% here means you answered 52 of 65 practice items correctly; it is not an estimate of your exam result.
