## 🎯 Why this course exists

Production multi-agent systems require more than fluent responses. Engineers must define **agent boundaries**, **tool authority**, **state ownership**, **failure handling**, **evaluation evidence**, and **release controls**.

These tests help you distinguish approaches that sound similar but solve different problems: **MCP versus A2A**, **retrieval relevance versus groundedness**, **prompt caching versus response caching**, **app-only identity versus delegated user access**, and **unit tests versus integration tests**.
