## ❓ Frequently asked questions

**Is this the real PCEP-30-03 exam?**
No. All 360 questions are original educational items authored for this course and verified on CPython 3.11. Nothing is copied or recalled from a live OpenEDG exam.

**What is the status of PCEP-30-03 vs PCEP-30-02?**
As checked on **October 29, 2026**, the OpenEDG Python Institute lists **PCEP-30-03** as **Pending Release (scheduled for October 31, 2026)** and states that it is a routine exam-version update under `PCEP-30-0x` that does not affect the five-year validity of credentials already earned. Because OpenEDG has not yet published a standalone `PCEP-30-03` syllabus table ahead of October 31, 2026, this bank baselines against the published `PCEP-30-0x` 18 / 29 / 25 / 28% syllabus and makes no claim about unpublished future blueprint changes. All 360 questions in this package are completely distinct from the `PCEP-30-02` package in this repository.

**Why are there 60 questions per practice test when the real exam has 30?**
Thirty items is a small sample across four syllabus blocks. Doubling each practice test to **60 items** lets every exam test all 42 study-map skills in exact **18 / 29 / 25 / 28** proportions, while doubling the clock from 40 minutes to **80 minutes** preserves the exact live pace of ~80 seconds per item.

**How are interactive gap-fill and drag-and-drop items handled?**
Udemy's player supports `multiple-choice` and `multi-select`. Each exam includes **16 to 18 single-blank `____` code-fill / gap-fill items** (minimum floor 15) formatted as multiple-choice, plus 15 five-option multiple-response items. Interactive drag-and-drop, sort, and multi-slot code-insertion widgets cannot exist in a Udemy CSV and are excluded.

**What is the passing score and retake policy?**
The Python Institute requires a **cumulative average score of at least 70%** across all exam blocks, with no separate per-block pass mark. Under TestNow™ policies, retakes require a **7-day waiting period** and unlimited retakes are allowed.

**Are classes, `pip`, or `open()` file streams tested here?**
No. Object-oriented programming, `pip`, file processing, closures, and decorators belong to **PCAP-31-03**, not entry-level PCEP, and are deliberately excluded.

**Why are there no case studies?**
Because the published `PCEP-30-0x` exam format has no case-study component.
