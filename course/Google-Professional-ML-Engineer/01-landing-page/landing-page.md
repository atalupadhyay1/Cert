## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Why 100 questions when the exam has 50–60?**
Because 100 items let each of the 47 study areas carry enough questions to be diagnosed, and 600 items let the blueprint be drilled twice without repeating a question. Every exam is labelled an expanded bank on its README, and no item count is attributed to Google.

**Q: Why are there no case studies?**
Because the Professional Machine Learning Engineer exam does not have a case-study component. Google's certification page and the exam guide describe 50–60 multiple-choice and multiple-select questions and nothing else. A case-study block here would imitate a different certification format, not this exam, so it is omitted — and the omission is stated rather than quietly filled.

**Q: What score do I need?**
Google does not publish a passing score for PMLE, so this course does not state one. Score each item correct or incorrect, group misses by local skill ID, and treat improvement per skill as the signal.

**Q: Why do some questions show configuration rather than theory?**
Because PMLE asks an engineer to read a design and decide what to change. Items show BigQuery ML SQL, AutoML budgets, notebook runtime settings, pipeline step definitions, endpoint traffic splits, autoscaling bounds, retraining triggers, and monitoring thresholds. Every one still tests judgement: what a change makes possible, what it prevents, and what it costs to run.

**Q: Is a Google Cloud account required?**
No. Every question is answerable from the stem and the options. No item requires you to train a model or deploy an endpoint.

**Q: How current is the content?**
The item bank follows the exam guide **as of June 1, 2026** and the certification page as retrieved in October 2026, including the Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform vocabulary that replaced the older Vertex AI naming (AutoML, Pipelines, Feature Store, Model Registry, Inference), plus Model Garden, Model Armor, Tabular Workflows, and Ray. Google changes these names often, so check the guide before your appointment.

**Q: Can I use this for the renewal exam?**
Not directly. Recertification is a separate, shorter instrument with its own scope. This bank is built for the standard Professional Machine Learning Engineer exam.
