### 3. Course Description

```text
Can you use Claude to get real business work done—and tell when its polished output should not be trusted?

Prepare for Claude Certified Associate – Foundations (CCAO-F) with 600 original single-answer questions across six tests of 100 questions each.

This is a business-practitioner exam, not a software-engineering exam. The practice focuses on defining tasks, prompting for usable results, evaluating accuracy and bias, choosing the right Claude features and model family, configuring Projects and knowledge, improving workflows, and applying governance, privacy, and responsible-use judgment.

WHAT YOU GET
• Six original 100-question tests
• Four alternatives and ONE best answer for every item
• An explanation for every option plus a case-specific overall rationale
• Official guide anchors and local study-map skill mappings
• Five focused learning tests followed by one integrated review
• Offline review copies alongside instructor CSV files

THE SIX TESTS

1. PROMPTING AND TASK EXECUTION
Write briefs with audience, constraints, evidence, and acceptance criteria; decompose dependent work; iterate with specific feedback; and adapt strategy to analysis, research, drafting, brainstorming, and planning.

2. OUTPUT EVALUATION AND VALIDATION
Check completeness, factual support, arithmetic, internal consistency, hallucination risk, unfair framing, citation support, and when qualified human review is required. Adapt and compare outputs for the intended audience and deliverable.

3. PRODUCT, MODEL, AND CONFIGURATION DECISIONS
Compare supported features such as Projects, chat, artifacts, and research; understand model-family positioning; align quality, speed, and cost; manage context and memory; and maintain Project instructions, knowledge sources, connectors, and change control.

4. WORKFLOW INTEGRATION AND SOLUTION DESIGN
Analyze requirements and handoffs, support research and process improvement, prototype safely, preserve approval and fallback controls, and communicate measured value with honest limitations.

5. GOVERNANCE, RISK, AND TROUBLESHOOTING
Assess appropriate use, data sensitivity, privacy, fairness, policy, and escalation. Diagnose weak prompts and poor outputs, adjust from feedback, handle missing or conflicting evidence, and optimize the whole workflow.

6. INTEGRATED ASSOCIATE SCENARIOS AND FINAL REVIEW
Apply all seven guide domains in an expanded mixed review: 14 prompting, 21 evaluation, 12 product/model, 16 workflow, 12 configuration, 15 governance, and 10 troubleshooting questions.
```
