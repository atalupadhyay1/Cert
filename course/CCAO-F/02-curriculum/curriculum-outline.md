# CCAO-F curriculum and study strategy

Baseline: official Exam Guide v1.0, effective July 2026; reviewed October 21, 2026. The portal announces an Associate – Foundations exam update on December 7, 2026, with an updated guide due November 17, 2026. This package does not claim coverage of the unpublished update. Recheck and revise before using it for that exam version.

## Course settings

- Six practice tests, 100 single-answer questions each; four alternatives per question.
- Optional custom duration: 200 minutes per test; optional study target: 80%.
- Read explanations after a first attempt; keep a mistake and uncertainty log.
- The actual exam has **60 items in 120 minutes** with multiple-choice and multiple-response items. These tests deliberately do not simulate that full format.

## Sequence and learning goals

### Test 1: Prompting and Task Execution

Define briefs, audiences, acceptance criteria, and source context; decompose dependent work; iterate with specific feedback; and choose strategies for analysis, research, drafting, and brainstorming.

Allocation: Prompting and Task Execution: 100.

[Instructor CSV](../03-practice-sets/set-1/practice-set-1.csv) · [Offline questions and explanations](../03-practice-sets/set-1/practice-set-1.md)

Review task: for three missed items, name the business constraint that decides the answer, then rewrite one prompt to add that constraint clearly.

### Test 2: Output Evaluation and Validation

Check accuracy, completeness, arithmetic, consistency, hallucination, bias, citation support, human-review need, audience adaptation, comparison design, and output format.

Allocation: Output Evaluation and Validation: 100.

[Instructor CSV](../03-practice-sets/set-2/practice-set-2.csv) · [Offline questions and explanations](../03-practice-sets/set-2/practice-set-2.md)

Review task: take one generated claim and document the exact evidence, scope, and date that would be required to support it.

### Test 3: Product, Model, and Configuration Decisions

Compare Projects, chats, artifacts, research, and supported access constraints; distinguish model-family positioning; align quality, speed, and cost; manage context and memory; and configure maintained Project instructions and knowledge.

Allocation: Product and Model Selection: 50, Configuration and Knowledge Management: 50.

[Instructor CSV](../03-practice-sets/set-3/practice-set-3.csv) · [Offline questions and explanations](../03-practice-sets/set-3/practice-set-3.md)

Review task: for a workflow you know, write the configuration, sources, permissions, and model criteria you would verify before use.

### Test 4: Workflow Integration and Solution Design

Analyze requirements and process handoffs, support research and planning, prototype and iterate safely, retain approval and fallback controls, and communicate measured value with limitations.

Allocation: Workflow Integration and Solution Design: 100.

[Instructor CSV](../03-practice-sets/set-4/practice-set-4.csv) · [Offline questions and explanations](../03-practice-sets/set-4/practice-set-4.md)

Review task: map one real workflow end to end, then mark where assistance is suitable and where authorization or expert review must remain.

### Test 5: Governance, Risk, and Troubleshooting

Judge appropriate use, data sensitivity, privacy, fairness, policy, and escalation; then diagnose prompt or output failures, adjust from feedback, handle missing evidence, and optimize the whole workflow.

Allocation: Governance, Risk, and Responsible Use: 60, Troubleshooting and Optimization: 40.

[Instructor CSV](../03-practice-sets/set-5/practice-set-5.csv) · [Offline questions and explanations](../03-practice-sets/set-5/practice-set-5.md)

Review task: write a one-page escalation note for a realistic risky request, including the policy owner and the safe next step.

### Test 6: Integrated Associate Scenarios and Final Review

Apply all seven guide domains in an expanded mixed review: 14 prompting, 21 evaluation, 12 product/model, 16 workflow, 12 configuration, 15 governance, and 10 troubleshooting questions.

Allocation: Domain 1: 14; Domain 2: 21; Domain 3: 12; Domain 4: 16; Domain 5: 12; Domain 6: 15; Domain 7: 10.

[Instructor CSV](../03-practice-sets/set-6/practice-set-6.csv) · [Offline questions and explanations](../03-practice-sets/set-6/practice-set-6.md)

Review task: attempt the set closed-book, then classify every miss as knowledge, evidence, or judgment error and schedule a focused retry.

## Study cadence

1. **Diagnose:** take one focused test without notes and log uncertain correct answers as well as misses.
2. **Repair:** revise the underlying skill using current official documentation and a fresh example.
3. **Apply:** test the idea on a small approved workflow, prototype, or Project configuration.
4. **Review:** attempt the integrated set later; do not treat repeated wording familiarity as readiness.
5. **Refresh:** confirm the current exam guide, format, fee, and version before scheduling.

## Coverage boundary

The package maps every local study ID to an official objective area, but does not claim equal depth in every subtopic, calibrated difficulty, psychometric equivalence, or that a practice score predicts a scaled exam result. Numeric IDs are local study aids; the official guide names objectives without this numbering.
