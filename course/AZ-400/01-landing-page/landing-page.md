## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Are these real or recalled AZ-400 exam questions?**
No. All 600 questions, 24 enterprise scenarios, and 277 option explanations are 100% original educational content authored from official Microsoft Learn and GitHub Docs specifications. Zero confidential items and zero Microsoft named case studies (`Contoso`, `Fabrikam`, `Tailwind Traders`, `Parts Unlimited`, etc.) appear anywhere in this course.

**Q: Why does each practice test have 100 questions when the live exam has 40–60?**
With 34 distinct DevOps sub-skills spanning both Azure DevOps and GitHub Enterprise, a 45-question test leaves half the blueprint untested per sitting. Expanding each mock exam to **100 blueprint-weighted items** guarantees all 34 skills are tested in every exam (`600` distinct items across the bank).

**Q: What is happening to Exam AZ-400 on February 1, 2027, and how does AZ-401 relate?**
Exam AZ-400 is **live today**. On October 15, 2026, Microsoft announced that **Exam AZ-400 (`DevOps Engineer Expert`) will retire on February 1, 2027**, succeeded by **Exam AZ-401 (`Designing and Implementing Microsoft Agentic DevOps Solutions` -> `Agentic DevOps Engineer Expert`)**. Candidates testing on or before February 1, 2027 take AZ-400; candidates targeting the new AI/MCP/Agentic DevOps track can sit AZ-401.

**Q: Do I need another certification to earn the DevOps Engineer Expert badge?**
Yes. Microsoft's Expert certification rule requires passing **Exam AZ-400** **AND** holding an active prerequisite Associate certification: **`Microsoft Certified: Azure Administrator Associate` (`AZ-104`)** or an active **`Microsoft Certified: Azure Developer Associate` (`AZ-204`, earned before July 31, 2026)**. You may take AZ-400 before or after your prerequisite exam, and the Expert credential issues once both requirements are active.

**Q: How does Microsoft's 700 / 1000 passing score work?**
Microsoft reports role-based exams on a `100–1000` scaled score where `700` is the passing cut score. It is **not** `70%` raw accuracy, and Microsoft adjusts for form difficulty and partial credit on multi-part items. Aim for consistent `80–85%+` accuracy across all five domains here and focus on mastering every distractor explanation.

**Q: Can I use Microsoft Learn during the real AZ-400 exam?**
Yes — Microsoft provides split-screen access to `learn.microsoft.com` during non-lab question sections of role-based exams (note that the exam clock keeps running, and `docs.github.com` or general web search are **not** accessible).
