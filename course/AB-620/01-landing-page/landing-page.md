## 🎯 Why this course exists

AB-620 is a builder exam with a documentation surface that is wide and shallow in places: topics, flows, tools, MCP, A2A, Foundry, Fabric, Application Insights, solutions, environments, pipelines. Most candidates can build one agent. Fewer can choose, under time pressure, between four options that are all real features of the product.

These questions are written as scenario decisions. Each one names a constraint — an on-premises system, an external audience, a regulated refusal, a throttled API, a reversible release — and only one option survives it. The explanations say why the other three fail, because that is the transferable part.

## 🧪 How the case studies work

Every mock exam contains four case studies of three questions. The scenario is written once and published in three forms:

| Route | File | Use it when |
|---|---|---|
| Study copy | `03-practice-sets/set-N/practice-set-N.md` | You want the scenario in full above each block, then the three questions |
| Brief bundle | `04-resources/case-studies.md` | You want all 24 scenarios with their questions and answer letters as one handout |
| Self-contained CSV | `03-practice-sets/set-N/case-embedded-set-N.csv` | Your platform shuffles items, so each case stem must carry the scenario |

The default `practice-set-N.csv` keeps stems short on purpose: a Udemy question field holds one block of text, and repeating a 70-word scenario three times per block reads badly on a phone.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Is this a dump of real exam questions?**
No. Every item is written for this course. Nothing here is recalled, reconstructed, or copied from a live exam, and no claim is made that these items will appear.

**How many questions are on the real exam?**
Microsoft does not publish a count for AB-620. Candidates commonly report 40–60; the practice exams here are 100 items each, an expanded bank so the blueprint can be drilled twice.

**Does a high practice score mean I will pass?**
No. Microsoft scores these exams on a scaled 0–1000 range with a 700 threshold, and difficulty weighting means a raw percentage has no published equivalent.

**Why are some "ordering" questions labelled multiple-choice?**
Udemy's practice-test importer accepts only `multiple-choice` and `multi-select`. Sequencing items are authored as four candidate sequences with one correct order, so they upload unchanged as multiple-choice.

**Why do the case studies repeat the scenario in some files?**
Because a practice-test player has no case-study container. You can keep stems short and publish the scenario as a resource, or embed the scenario in each stem for platforms that shuffle items.

**Are the questions updated when Microsoft changes the outline?**
The package pins the outline it was built against (16 October 2026) and the build fails if a case name or item count drifts. Re-check the live study guide before you book, and rebuild if the weights change.
