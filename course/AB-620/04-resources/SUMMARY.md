# AB-620 package summary

This course covers **Microsoft Certified: AI Agent Builder Associate (AB-620)**, the exam *Designing and Building Integrated AI Agent Solutions in Copilot Studio*.

- Six mock exams × 100 original questions = **600**.
- Every exam is blueprint-weighted **33 / 42 / 25** percent, inside all three published ranges (30–35 / 40–45 / 20–25).
- Item mix per exam: **76 multiple-choice, 16 multi-select**, plus **4 case studies of 3 questions**. Eight of the multiple-choice items are sequencing items with four candidate sequences.
- Multi-select stems state the required count; sequencing items offer four candidate sequences and export as `multiple-choice`, because the Udemy importer accepts only `multiple-choice` and `multi-select`.
- Three official domains, 30 local study-map skills, all mapped in exports.
- Per-option explanations plus a case rationale, study-guide anchor, local skill, and primary reference on every item.
- Live exam baseline: Microsoft does not publish an item count; 40–60 questions in 120 minutes is commonly reported, 700/1000 scaled pass, $165 USD, proctored with possible interactive components and preview features, annual renewal through a free assessment. Study guide measured as of October 16, 2026.
- Each exam is 100 items, deliberately larger than the commonly reported 40–60, so the blueprint can be drilled twice; it is an expanded bank, not a length replica.
- Not reproduced: hot-area items, drag-and-drop UI, labs and live tenant components, and the exact live item count.
- Offline ZIP: `AB-620-AI-Agent-Builder-Associate-6x100.zip`.

Automated checks cover structure, blueprint allocation, item-type mix, source parity, explanation pairing, CSV bytes, cross-exam duplicate wording, case-study block shape, and archive reproducibility. They do not establish independent technical accuracy, psychometric quality, platform import success, or a passing result.

A practice percentage is not a scaled exam score. Microsoft does not publish a raw percentage equivalent to 700/1000.
