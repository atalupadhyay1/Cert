## Known limits

- The exam is new relative to older Microsoft certifications, and its preview-era features can change. Re-check the guide before scheduling.
- Product behaviour that differs between regions, licensing tiers, or preview rings is described at the level the exam outline supports, not at the level of a tenant-specific configuration.
- Semantic duplicate detection is normalized-text based, not embedding based.
