# AB-731 package summary

This course covers **Microsoft Certified: AI Transformation Leader (AB-731)**.

- Six mock exams × 100 original questions = **600**.
- Every exam is blueprint-weighted **37 / 38 / 25** = 37 / 38 / 25 percent, inside all three published ranges (35–40 / 35–40 / 20–25).
- Item mix per exam: **76 multiple-choice, 16 multi-select**, plus **4 case studies of 3 questions**. Eight of the multiple-choice items are sequencing items with four candidate sequences.
- Multi-select stems state the required count; sequencing items offer four candidate sequences and export as `multiple-choice`, because the importer accepts only `multiple-choice` and `multi-select`.
- Three official domains, 30 local study-map skills, all mapped in exports.
- Per-option explanations plus a case rationale, study-guide anchor, local skill, and primary reference on every item.
- Assessment baseline: **45 minutes**, proctored, **no published item count**, 700/1000 scaled pass, $99 USD, 13 languages, no coding, valid 1 year with free renewal, study guide measured as of **July 22, 2026**, Microsoft practice assessment and exam sandbox available.
- Each exam is 100 items, deliberately larger than a 45-minute assessment can be, so the blueprint can be drilled twice; it is an expanded bank, not a length replica.
- Not reproduced: hot-area items, drag-and-drop UI, labs and interactive components, adaptive problem-solution sets, and the exact live item count.
- Offline ZIP: `AB-731-AI-Transformation-Leader-6x100.zip`.

Automated checks cover structure, blueprint allocation, item-type mix, source parity, explanation pairing, CSV bytes, cross-exam duplicate wording, and archive reproducibility. They do not establish independent business accuracy, psychometric quality, platform import success, or a passing result.

A practice percentage is not a scaled exam score. Microsoft does not publish a raw percentage equivalent to 700/1000.
