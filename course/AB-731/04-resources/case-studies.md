# AB-731 case study briefs

Every mock exam contains four case studies of three questions. The scenario below is the shared context;
the three items that follow it are scored individually. Paste the relevant section into a Udemy lecture,
a downloadable resource, or the practice-test description when you want the scenario available before the questions.

## Exam 1

### Case study — Adatum Bank

Adatum Bank is a retail and commercial bank with 22,000 staff and a procurement function that handles 9,000 supplier contracts. A procurement assistant summarizes uploaded contracts, and one supplier document contained text instructing the model to ignore its rules. Separately, a screening assistant shortlisted candidates for branch roles, and the review found it favoured one demographic. The bank is also about to pilot an assistant that will affect how credit applications are prioritized, and group risk wants that assessed before launch.

Questions in this block

1. A procurement assistant summarizes uploaded supplier contracts, and one contract contains text instructing the model to ignore its rules. What risk must the design address? _(D)_
2. A screening assistant shortlists candidates for branch roles and the review finds it favours one university consistently. Which responsible AI principle is at stake? _(A)_
3. A pilot will affect how credit applications are prioritized. What must be completed before the pilot starts? _(C)_

### Case study — Contoso Health

Contoso Health is a hospital group with a clinical workforce of 14,000 and a Microsoft 365 tenant used for rosters and HR administration. A workforce assistant will answer from HR files that include individual salary letters alongside policy documents. The quality team also wants a triage assistant that recommends which patients receive a follow-up call, where an error changes clinical priority. Copilot is due to be enabled across clinical SharePoint sites next month, and permissions have not been reviewed for years.

Questions in this block

1. A workforce assistant will answer from HR files that include individual salary letters alongside general policy. Which control is essential before launch? _(D)_
2. A triage system recommends which patients get a follow-up call, and an error changes clinical priority. What must the design keep? _(C)_
3. The hospital plans to enable Copilot across its clinical SharePoint sites next month and has never reviewed permissions. What must happen first? _(C)_

### Case study — Fabrikam Retail

Fabrikam Retail runs 300 stores plus an online business, with sales history in a warehouse and merchandising decisions taken weekly. Store operations wants next quarter's demand per store from three years of transactions, and the buying team wants 400 seasonal policy questions answered on the returns portal. The merchandising team edits the assistant prompt every week and cannot say whether answers improved. Marketing budget is under review, so every capability has to justify its cost against a defined business outcome.

Questions in this block

1. Store operations wants next quarter's demand for each of 300 stores based on three years of sales history. Which AI approach fits best? _(D)_
2. The returns portal sends the same 400 policy questions to the model thousands of times a day and the answers are identical. What should be added? _(C)_
3. The merchandising team changes the prompt every week and cannot tell whether answers are improving or getting worse. What should be introduced? _(D)_

### Case study — Northwind Insurance

Northwind Insurance processes 20,000 supplier invoices a month as PDFs and employs a claims shared service of 120 people. The claims assistant answers from a policy library that is revised quarterly, and adjusters have seen it invent a clause when nothing relevant was retrieved. Twelve thousand notifications a month are routine, while 600 cases need genuine judgement. The claims director wants automation where volume is high and human attention where the consequence is a payment decision.

Questions in this block

1. The claims shared service receives 20,000 supplier invoices a month as PDFs and must post the totals, dates, and tax codes into the finance system. Which solution fits best? _(A)_
2. The claims assistant answers confidently even when the policy library has nothing relevant, and staff have started trusting those answers. What should be added? _(B)_
3. The claims team handles 12,000 routine notifications a month and 600 genuinely complex cases. Where should automation be applied? _(C)_
