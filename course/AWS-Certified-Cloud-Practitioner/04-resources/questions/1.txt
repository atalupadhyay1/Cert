1.8|S|multi_az_deploy|single_az_design,monolith_scale,tight_coupling|A checkout service currently runs in one data-centre building and stops when that building loses power. What should be done?|Deploying across multiple Availability Zones leaves a survivor when one Zone fails.

2.2|S|access_key_rotation|root_mfa_delete,iam_mfa,iam_policy_json|An audit finds a programmatic access key that has not changed in three years and is used by a reporting script. What should be done?|A rotation schedule limits how long a leaked key stays useful, and old keys stop working.
2.3|S|iam_role|iam_user,root_use_daily,hardcoded_secret|An application on Amazon EC2 must read objects from a bucket without any credential being stored on the instance. What should be used?|A role supplies temporary credentials to the workload, so nothing long-lived is written to disk.
