# CLF-C02 package summary

This course covers the **AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner (CLF-C02)** certification.

- Six mock exams × 65 original questions = **390**.
- Every exam is blueprint-weighted **16 / 19 / 22 / 8** against the published **24 / 30 / 34 / 12** percent. Those four weights total exactly 100; applied to 65 items they give 15.6, 19.5, 22.1 and 7.8, and the per-exam counts resolve the two exact halves towards a total of 65 — security rounds down to 19, billing rounds up to 8. The tests assert those exact counts, so a future weight change fails the build.
- Item mix per exam: **55 multiple-choice, 10 multiple-response**. Multiple-response items use five options and state the required count in the stem (2 / 3 / 4 / 1 across domains 1–4). **AWS publishes no split between the two item types**, so this mix is an authoring choice and is labelled as one in every set README, in the review copies, and on the landing page.
- **65 items is the live paper length.** AWS publishes 65 questions in 90 minutes, of which 50 are scored and 15 are unscored and unidentified. At 65 items in 90 minutes the bank runs at about **83 seconds per item**, the same pace as the appointment, so a timed run is a genuine rehearsal. The one honest difference: a practice percentage here measures all 65 items, while 15 live items do not count.
- **No case studies.** The published CLF-C02 format is multiple choice and multiple response with no case-study component, so the bank has no `Case study —` stems, no `cases.py`, and no case-study documents. `build.py` refuses to build if a case phrase reaches a stem or rationale, `validate.py` rejects case wording in an export, and `package.py` rejects a reinstated case-study file.
- **Concept and service recognition only.** AWS lists coding, architecture design, troubleshooting, implementation, and load and performance testing as out of scope. Items describe a business requirement and ask which concept or service fits, or which choice would fail it. No item is a console recipe and none needs an AWS account; the test suite rejects console-instruction phrasing (`click`, `navigate to`, `in the console, select`).
- **No sequencing or drag-and-drop items.** Those formats are not part of the published CLF-C02 format.
- Four official domains and **37 local study-map skills**, all exercised, mapped in every export.
- Per-option explanations plus a rationale, exam-guide reference, local skill, and primary reference on every item.
- Exam baseline: **90 minutes**, **100 USD**, **65 questions (50 scored + 15 unscored)**, multiple choice and multiple response, Pearson VUE test centre or online proctoring, **12 languages** with German and Italian retiring after **31 December 2026**, **no prerequisites** (typical candidate has up to six months of AWS exposure; no IT background required), and a **scaled score of 100–1000 with a minimum passing score of 700** under compensatory scoring — all AWS-published on the certification page and in the guide. Certification is valid for **three years**, and AWS publishes Cloud Quest, a retake, and passing an Associate or Professional exam as recertification paths.
- **700 is stated as published and decoupled from practice percentages.** It is a scaled score, not 70% of 65, so no target percentage appears anywhere and no practice result is converted into a scaled score.
- Product vocabulary follows the CLF-C02 exam guide: Regions, Availability Zones, edge locations, Local Zones, Wavelength and Outposts; IAM users, groups, roles, policies, Identity Center and federation, with Cognito for customer identity; security groups, network ACLs, WAF, Shield and Network Firewall; KMS, customer managed keys, S3 server-side encryption, Certificate Manager and Secrets Manager; GuardDuty, Inspector, Macie, Security Hub, Detective, Artifact, Config, CloudTrail, Audit Manager and Trusted Advisor; Organizations service control policies, Control Tower and Service Catalog; EC2, Lambda, ECS and EKS with Fargate, Elastic Beanstalk, Lightsail, Batch and App Runner; S3 storage classes, EBS, EFS, FSx, Storage Gateway, Snowball and Backup; RDS, Aurora, DynamoDB, Redshift, ElastiCache, Neptune and DocumentDB; VPC, Route 53, CloudFront, Direct Connect, Site-to-Site VPN, Elastic Load Balancing, API Gateway, Transit Gateway, PrivateLink and Global Accelerator; CloudWatch, CloudFormation, Systems Manager and the Health Dashboard; SQS, SNS, EventBridge, Step Functions, Kinesis and Amazon MQ; Athena, Glue, EMR, QuickSight, SageMaker and Bedrock; CodePipeline, CodeBuild, CodeDeploy, Cloud9 and X-Ray; On-Demand, Reserved Instances, Spot Instances, Savings Plans and Dedicated Hosts; Cost Explorer, Budgets, the Cost and Usage Report, Pricing Calculator, Cost Anomaly Detection, cost allocation tags and consolidated billing; Basic, Developer, Business and Enterprise Support with technical account managers; the Free Tier and the AWS Partner Network.
- Not reproduced: lab tasks, in-console exercises, and any interactive item format, because they cannot exist in a CSV and are not part of the published format.
- Offline ZIP: `AWS-Certified-Cloud-Practitioner-6x65.zip`.

Automated checks cover structure, exact blueprint allocation, per-skill plan, multiple-response spread, item-type mix, source parity, explanation pairing, CSV bytes, cross-exam duplicate wording, a token-overlap near-duplicate gate at 0.72 across all 390 stems, the absence of case wording, the absence of console-recipe phrasing, and archive reproducibility. They do not establish independent technical accuracy, psychometric quality, platform import success, or a passing result.

Practice percentages are not scaled scores, and passing the real exam requires a scaled 700 that this course cannot predict.
