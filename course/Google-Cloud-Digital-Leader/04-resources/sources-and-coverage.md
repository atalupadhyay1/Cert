## What is deliberately not covered

- **Configuration tasks.** There is no configuration objective in the guide, so no item asks for a resource setting, a policy body, a role binding, or a tuning parameter. This applies to every domain, not as an exception but as the exam's own scope.
- **Case studies.** Not part of the published format.
- **Hands-on labs and console exercises.** Not part of the published format, and not representable in a CSV.
- **Renewal-exam calibration.** The 20-question renewal instrument is a different, shorter paper. This bank targets the standard exam.

## Source discipline

- No question, option, or explanation is copied from any question-sharing site, braindump collection, or community forum.
- Community statistics from such sites — item counts, "found the real exam almost the same" percentages, self-reported pass counts, average-score banners — are **not** used as evidence, benchmarks, or marketing claims anywhere in this course.
- Where Google publishes a range (50–60 questions) or declines to publish a value (the passing score), this course repeats the range or says the value is unpublished. It never fills the gap with an estimate of its own.
- Product names follow the **August 12, 2026** guide. Older names were not carried forward, and terms were not invented for products that do not exist.
