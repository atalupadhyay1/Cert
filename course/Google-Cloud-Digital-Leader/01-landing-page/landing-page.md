## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Why 100 questions when the exam has 50–60 and only 90 minutes?**
Because 100 items let each of the 41 study areas carry enough questions to be diagnosed, and 600 items let the blueprint be drilled twice without repeating a question. The consequence is honest: 100 items in 90 minutes is about 54 seconds per item, while the live 50–60 item paper gives 90–108 seconds. This bank therefore runs **faster** than the real exam. Sit it untimed first, then use the clock as stricter practice. Every exam is labelled an expanded bank on its README, and no item count is attributed to Google.

**Q: Why are there no case studies?**
Because the Cloud Digital Leader exam does not have a case-study component. Google's certification page and the exam guide describe 50–60 multiple-choice and multiple-select questions and nothing else. A case-study block here would imitate a different certification format, not this exam, so it is omitted — and the omission is stated rather than quietly filled.

**Q: Why is there no configuration content?**
Because Google's CDL guide contains no configuration objective. The exam assesses whether you can recognise the business value of a technology, match a product to a stated need, and explain a risk in business terms. There is no "configuration is in scope" exception to document for this exam, and no item asks you to design a subnet, write a policy, or tune a resource — that would misrepresent the paper.

**Q: What score do I need?**
Google does not publish a passing score for CDL, so this course does not state one. Score each item correct or incorrect, group misses by local skill ID, and treat improvement per skill as the signal.

**Q: Is a Google Cloud account required?**
No. Every question is answerable from the stem and the options. No item requires you to open the console.

**Q: What is the renewal path?**
CDL is valid for **3 years**. Google offers a **45-minute, $60, 20-question renewal exam** in English and Japanese, or you can complete designated Google Skills courses and badges instead. This bank is built for the **standard** exam and is **not** calibrated to the 20-question renewal instrument.

**Q: How current is the content?**
The item bank follows the exam guide **launched August 12, 2026** and the certification page as retrieved in October 2026. That revision added current vocabulary — agentic AI, Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform, Agent Studio and AutoML on Agent Platform, AI Hypercomputer, Cloud Run functions, Managed Service for Apache Spark, Google Threat Intelligence, Google Security Operations, AI Protection, Model Armor, Gemini in Security Operations, Certificate Manager, AlloyDB Omni, BigQuery Omni, GKE Enterprise, Autoclass, and Database Migration Service — so the publication checklist includes a product-name re-check before release.
