HOW THE ITEM MIX MATCHES THE REAL EXAM
The live role-based exam is not a wall of definitions. Microsoft states that PL-300 is typically 40–60 questions in 100 minutes, and candidates routinely see case studies, multi-select items, and scenario questions about a concrete model or report.

These mock exams reproduce what a CSV player can score honestly:
• Single-choice items with one best answer.
• Multi-select items that state the required count ("Select two") and accept a comma-separated key.
• Ordering items rendered as four candidate sequences, so sequencing judgment is tested without a drag-and-drop UI.
• Case studies: a shared scenario followed by three scored questions, exactly as a case-study block behaves in the exam.

What is deliberately not reproduced, and is stated in every export: hot-area image selection, drag-and-drop UI, labs and interactive components, adaptive problem-solution (yes/no) sets, and the exact live item count.

HOW THE FILES ARE EXPORTED
Udemy's bulk question importer accepts only two `Question Type` values: `multiple-choice` and `multi-select`. Every file here therefore exports 84 multiple-choice and 16 multi-select items per exam. The eight sequencing items are authored as four candidate orderings with one correct answer, so they upload as ordinary multiple-choice items — no drag-and-drop UI is required and scoring stays exact.

WHY EACH EXAM IS 100 ITEMS
A 100-item exam is longer than the live 40–60. It is an expanded practice bank: the extra volume gives every sub-objective real coverage and lets you drill the same blueprint twice without repeating a question. ## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How many questions are included?**

A: **600 across six exams of 100**, with 76 multiple-choice, 16 multi-select, and 4 case studies of 3 questions per exam. Eight of the multiple-choice items per exam ask you to choose the correct candidate sequence.

**Q: Is every exam blueprint-weighted?**

A: Yes. All six use **28 / 28 / 28 / 16**, which is 28 / 28 / 28 / 16 percent — inside every published range (25–30 / 25–30 / 25–30 / 15–20).

**Q: Why 100 questions when the exam is 40–60?**

A: So every sub-objective gets real coverage and each blueprint can be drilled twice without repeats. It is an expanded bank, not a length replica, and the package says so rather than implying the live exam is longer.

**Q: Do these reproduce the actual exam?**

A: No, and the package says so. The live exam is typically 40–60 questions with drag-and-drop and hot-area formats. Practice exams are 100 items and reproduce the scoreable formats: single-choice, multi-select, ordering as candidate sequences, and case studies.

**Q: Why are hot-area, drag-and-drop, and labs missing?**

A: They need an interactive or provisioned environment. A CSV cannot render a hotspot, a drag gesture, or a lab sandbox, and faking them would break scoring. They are excluded and disclosed in every export.

**Q: Do I need Power BI installed?**

A: No. The questions test judgment about models, DAX behaviour, visuals, and the service. Having Desktop open alongside is useful for the Power Query and modelling items, but nothing in the package requires it.

**Q: How are multi-select answers scored?**

A: The `Correct Answers` column lists every correct option index as a comma-separated value, for example `2,4`. The stem states the count ("Select two"), so a learner knows how many to choose. There is no partial credit.

**Q: How are sequencing questions scored and exported?**

A: Four candidate sequences are offered as options A–D, and exactly one is in the correct order. That tests the same sequencing judgment as a drag-and-drop item without requiring a drag-and-drop player, and it imports as an ordinary multiple-choice item.

**Q: Is there a passing score?**

A: The real exam passes at **700/1000** on a scaled score. Microsoft does not publish a raw percentage equivalent, so this package does not claim one. Any percentage target you choose is a study convention.

**Q: Does the package assume a Fabric capacity or a premium licence?**

A: No. Where a capability depends on capacity, tenant settings, or licensing, the item asks you to reason about the requirement and the explanation names the dependency.

**Q: Are the numeric skill IDs official?**

A: No. The study guide names objectives without this numbering. Local IDs make coverage auditable.

**Q: Is this an official Microsoft course?**

A: No. All items are original independent educational practice, not actual or recalled content, and this course is not endorsed by Microsoft.
