## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Why 100 questions when the exam has 50–60?**
Because 100 items let each of the 42 study areas carry enough questions to be diagnosed, and 600 items let the blueprint be drilled twice without repeating a question. Every exam is labelled an expanded bank on its README, and no item count is attributed to Google.

**Q: What score do I need?**
Google does not publish a passing score for PCA, so this course does not state one. Score each item correct or incorrect, group misses by local skill ID, and treat improvement per skill as the signal.

**Q: Does this include the official case studies?**
No. Google names four case studies in the exam guide (Altostrat Media, Cymbal Retail, EHR Healthcare, and KnightMotives Automotive) and publishes them on the certification page for candidates to read. They are **not** reproduced here and no item is derived from them. The 24 case studies in this bank are original companies with original constraints.

**Q: Why do some questions show configuration rather than theory?**
Because PCA asks an architect to read a design and decide what to change. Items show IAM conditions, organization policy constraints, deployment rules, subnet and routing choices, lifecycle settings, checkpoint and watermark patterns, and Terraform delivery pipelines. Every one still tests judgement: what a change makes possible, what it prevents, and what it costs to run.

**Q: Is a Google Cloud account required?**
No. Every question is answerable from the stem and the options. No item requires you to create a resource.

**Q: How current is the content?**
The item bank follows the exam guide and certification page as retrieved in October 2026, including the rebranded product names used in the current guide (Gemini Cloud Assist, Agent Platform, Model Armor, AI Hypercomputer, Chrome Enterprise Premium). Google changes these names often, so check the guide before your appointment.

**Q: Can I use this for the renewal exam?**
Not directly. The renewal exam is one hour, 25 questions, and one generative-AI case study worth 90–100% of the score. That is a different instrument, and this bank is built for the standard exam.
