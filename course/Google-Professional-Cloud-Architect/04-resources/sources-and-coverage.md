## Verified exam facts

| Fact | Value |
|---|---|
| Domains and weights | 1) Designing and planning a cloud solution architecture ~25%; 2) Managing and provisioning a solution infrastructure ~17.5%; 3) Designing for security and compliance ~17.5%; 4) Analyzing and optimizing technical and business processes ~15%; 5) Managing implementation ~12.5%; 6) Ensuring solution and operations excellence ~12.5% |
| Length | 2 hours |
| Fee | $200 USD plus applicable tax |
| Format | 50–60 multiple choice and multiple select questions |
| Case studies | Each exam includes 2 case studies; case-study questions are 20–30% of the score; 4 case studies are listed in the guide |
| Passing score | **Not published** — the exam reports pass or fail |
| Languages | English and Japanese |
| Validity | 2 years |
| Delivery | Online proctored or at a test centre |
| Prerequisites | None; 3+ years of industry experience including 1+ year on Google Cloud is recommended |
| Renewal | Standard exam, a 1-hour 25-question renewal exam, or designated courses and skill badges |

The length, fee, question range, and case-study weighting above are all Google-published on the certification page; the **passing score is not published**, and this package therefore never states a target percentage or converts a practice score into a pass mark.

Not claimed anywhere in this package: that the practice exams contain the live item count, that they reproduce the split-screen case-study panel, drag-and-drop ordering, or lab tasks, that they reuse the official case studies, or that completing the course earns the certification.

## Deliberate omissions and disclosures

| Excluded element | Why it is excluded | How the package compensates |
|---|---|---|
| The four official case studies | They are Google's published material, not ours | 24 original scenarios of five questions each, all with invented companies |
| Split-screen case-study panel | A player feature, not a CSV field | The scenario is repeated in each case-item stem in the `case-embedded` variant, and printed once per block in the review copy |
| Drag-and-drop ordering | Requires a drag-and-drop player | Sequencing is authored as four candidate orders with one correct order, exported as multiple choice |
| Lab and interactive console tasks | Require a live project | Configuration is presented as text and read, not typed |
| Live item count | The live exam samples 50–60 questions | Each exam is labelled a 100-item expanded bank |
| Passing score | Google does not publish one | No target percentage is stated anywhere |

## Licensing, capability, and pricing statements

Every item is answerable without a project. Where a scenario depends on a capability that requires a paid edition, a specific region, or an enterprise tier, the explanation names that dependency rather than implying the capability is free or universal. No item asserts a current price, quota, or regional availability; the only price in the package is the published $200 registration fee, cited as published and subject to change.
