## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is AB-100 a fundamentals exam?**
A: No. AB-100 is an **expert-level** exam. It requires an active Associate-level prerequisite certification (Dynamics 365 functional consultant, Power Platform developer/functional consultant, Azure AI Engineer Associate, or one of the other qualifying credentials). AI-900 and AB-900 are fundamentals-level, while AB-100 expects 2-3 years of solution architect experience.

**Q: How many questions will the real exam have?**
A: Microsoft does not publish an AB-100-specific question count, but typically Microsoft expert-level exams have 40-60 questions plus at least one case study. This course gives you 600 across 6 sets — far more than you need to be exam-ready.

**Q: How long is the real exam?**
A: 100 minutes (additional 30 minutes available for non-native English speakers on request). We recommend completing Set 5 (full-length simulation) inside 100 minutes to practice real exam pacing.

**Q: What's the passing score?**
A: 700 out of 1000 (scaled — not a flat 70%).

**Q: Does the certification expire?**
A: Yes. Microsoft 365 expert certifications expire every 12 months. You can renew by passing a free online assessment on Microsoft Learn.

**Q: Are these questions copied from Microsoft?**
A: No. Every question is original, written from the official Microsoft Learn documentation, and every explanation cites a Microsoft Learn URL.

**Q: Does the exam really have 40-45% Deploy questions?**
A: Yes. Deploy is the largest domain. If you are weak in Deploy (ALM, telemetry, responsible AI, security, governance), spend extra time on Set 4.

**Q: What are MCP and A2A, and why do they matter?**
A: MCP (Model Context Protocol) and A2A (Agent2Agent) are open standards for connecting agents to tools and to other agents. They are core AB-100 exam topics. See https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-copilot-studio/mcp-overview for MCP and the [Agent2Agent protocol](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-copilot-studio/a2a-overview) for A2A.
