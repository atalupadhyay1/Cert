# AB-100 Practice Test Course — Curriculum Outline & Strategy

> **Complete curriculum blueprint for all 6 practice test sets.** Each set is 100 questions, mapped to the official AB-100 exam objective domains (skills measured as of October 14, 2026).

## 📊 OFFICIAL AB-100 EXAM DOMAIN BREAKDOWN

| Domain | Exam Weight | Practice Coverage |
|---|---|---|
| **Plan AI-powered business solutions** | 25–30% | Sets 1, 2, 5, 6 |
| **Design AI-powered business solutions** | 25–30% | Sets 1, 3, 5, 6 |
| **Deploy AI-powered business solutions** | 40–45% | Sets 1, 4, 5, 6 (heaviest) |

> ℹ️ **Question Pool Logic:** Because Deploy is 40-45% of the real exam, every set has **at least 25 questions** from Deploy/ALM/responsible AI/security/governance. Plan and Design alternate as the secondary emphasis. Sets 2-4 are domain deep dives; Sets 5-6 are mixed sims.
