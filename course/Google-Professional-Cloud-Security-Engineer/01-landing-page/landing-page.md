## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Why 100 questions when the exam has 50–60?**
Because 100 items let each of the 48 study areas carry enough questions to be diagnosed, and 600 items let the blueprint be drilled twice without repeating a question. Every exam is labelled an expanded bank on its README, and no item count is attributed to Google.

**Q: Why are there no case studies?**
Because the Professional Cloud Security Engineer exam does not have a case-study component. Google's certification page and the exam guide describe 50–60 multiple-choice and multiple-select questions and nothing else. A case-study block here would imitate a different certification format, not this exam, so it is omitted — and the omission is stated rather than quietly filled.

**Q: What score do I need?**
Google does not publish a passing score for PCSE, so this course does not state one. Score each item correct or incorrect, group misses by local skill ID, and treat improvement per skill as the signal.

**Q: Why do some questions show configuration rather than theory?**
Because PCSE asks an engineer to read a design and decide what to change. Items show firewall policy attachment points, IAM conditions and deny policies, access levels, perimeter and restricted VIP choices, lifecycle and retention settings, key ring and rotation settings, log sink configurations, and admission policy behaviour. Every one still tests judgement: what a change makes possible, what it prevents, and what it costs to operate.

**Q: Is a Google Cloud account required?**
No. Every question is answerable from the stem and the options. No item requires you to create a perimeter or rotate a key.

**Q: How current is the content?**
The item bank follows the exam guide and the certification page as retrieved in October 2026. That page describes a role that now includes security automation, AI workload protection, software supply chain security, and regulatory controls, so the publication checklist includes a product-name and scope re-check before release.

**Q: Can I use this for the renewal exam?**
Not directly. Recertification is a separate, shorter instrument with its own scope. This bank is built for the standard Professional Cloud Security Engineer exam.
