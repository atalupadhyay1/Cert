# Official Microsoft Learn Sources & 36-Skill Coverage Map (Exam PL-400 / AB-400)

## Official Credential & Blueprint Baseline

- **Exam:** `Exam PL-400: Microsoft Power Platform Developer` (`https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/exams/pl-400/`)
- **Credential Earned:** `Microsoft Certified: Power Platform Developer Associate` (`https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/power-platform-developer-associate/`) — Associate-level certification with **no prerequisite exam required**, **`700 / 1000`** scaled passing score, `40–60` live questions in `100` minutes, and free 12-month online renewal on Microsoft Learn.
- **October 16, 2026 Study Guide & October 30, 2026 PL-400 -> AB-400 Transition:** Aligned to the official **October 16, 2026** Microsoft Learn PL-400 study guide (`https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/resources/study-guides/pl-400`). Registration for `PL-400` closed October 16, 2026, with final scheduled exams delivered through **October 30, 2026** (11:59 PM CST), transitioning directly to **Exam AB-400: Extending Microsoft Power Platform Solutions with Code and AI** (`https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/resources/study-guides/ab-400`) on the identical 4-domain blueprint.
