# AIP-C01 package summary

This course covers the **AWS Certified Generative AI Developer - Professional (AIP-C01)** certification.

- Six mock exams × 75 original questions = **450**.
- Every exam is blueprint-weighted **23 / 20 / 15 / 9 / 8** against the published **31 / 26 / 20 / 12 / 11** percent. Those five weights total exactly 100; applied to 75 items they give 23.25 / 19.5 / 15 / 9 / 8.25, and largest-remainder rounding gives the surplus item to Domain 2, whose exact share is the half item. Every count is within 0.5 items of its published weight, and the tests assert those exact counts so a weight change fails the build.
- Item mix per exam: **60 multiple-choice, 15 multiple-response**. Multiple-response items use five options and state the required count in the stem (3 / 3 / 3 / 3 / 3 across domains 1–5). **AWS publishes no split between the two item types**, so this mix is an authoring choice and is labelled as one in every set README, in the review copies, and on the landing page.
- **75 items is the published paper length.** The exam guide defines 75 questions in 180 minutes, of which 65 are scored and 10 are unscored and unidentified. At 75 items in 180 minutes the bank runs at about **144 seconds per item**, the same pace as a scored sitting. The one honest difference: a practice percentage here measures all 75 items, while 10 live items do not count.
- **No case studies.** The published format is multiple choice and multiple response with no case-study component, so the bank has no `Case study —` stems, no `cases.py`, and no case-study documents. `build.py` refuses to build if a case phrase reaches a stem or rationale, `validate.py` rejects case wording in an export, and `package.py` rejects a reinstated case-study file.
- **Integration depth, not model training.** The guide places model development and training, advanced ML techniques, and data engineering and feature engineering out of scope. Items ask which store, chunk split, retrieval control, model, agent pattern, guardrail, metric or cost lever fits a stated requirement. No item is a console recipe and none needs an AWS account; the test suite rejects console-instruction phrasing (`click`, `navigate to`, `in the console, select`).
- **No sequencing or drag-and-drop items.** Those formats are not part of the published format.
- Five official domains and **45 local study-map skills**, all exercised, mapped in every export.
- Per-option explanations plus a rationale, exam-guide reference, local skill, and primary reference on every item.
- Exam baseline: **180 minutes**, **300 USD**, **75 questions (65 scored + 10 unscored)**, multiple choice and multiple response, Pearson VUE test centre or online proctoring, **English, Japanese, Korean and Simplified Chinese**, a **scaled score of 100–1000 with a minimum passing score of 750** under compensatory scoring with no per-domain pass mark, and **three-year validity** — all AWS-published on the certification page and in the guide. AWS recommends two or more years building production-grade applications plus one year of hands-on generative AI work.
- **750 is stated as published and decoupled from practice percentages.** It is a scaled score, not a percentage of 75, so no target percentage appears anywhere and no practice result is converted into a scaled score.
- Product vocabulary follows the AIP-C01 exam guide's in-scope service list: Amazon Bedrock with knowledge bases, prompt management, prompt flows, guardrails, model evaluation, provisioned throughput, cross-Region inference, batch inference and AgentCore; Strands Agents, AWS Agent Squad and the Model Context Protocol; OpenSearch, Aurora with pgvector, Neptune, DocumentDB, DynamoDB, ElastiCache, RDS and S3; Glue and Glue Data Quality, Kinesis, MSK, Athena, EMR and Quick Sight; Lambda, Lambda@Edge, EC2, App Runner, Outposts and Wavelength; ECS, EKS, Fargate and ECR; API Gateway, AppSync, CloudFront, Elastic Load Balancing, Global Accelerator, Route 53, PrivateLink and VPC; Step Functions, EventBridge, SQS, SNS, AppFlow and AppConfig; CloudFormation, CDK, CodePipeline, CodeBuild, CodeDeploy, CodeArtifact, X-Ray and Q Developer; Comprehend, Kendra, Lex, Q Business, Rekognition, Transcribe, Textract, Titan, Augmented AI and SageMaker AI with Model Registry, Model Monitor, Clarify, Processing, Ground Truth, JumpStart, Neo and Unified Studio; CloudWatch with metrics, logs, Logs Insights and Synthetics, Managed Grafana, CloudTrail, Cost Explorer, Cost Anomaly Detection, Systems Manager, Service Catalog, Chatbot and the Well-Architected Tool; IAM with Access Analyzer and Identity Center, Cognito, KMS, Secrets Manager, Macie, WAF and the Encryption SDK; S3 Intelligent-Tiering and Lifecycle policies; DataSync and Transfer Family.
- Not reproduced: lab tasks, in-console exercises, and any interactive item format, because they cannot exist in a CSV and are not part of the published format.
- Offline ZIP: `AWS-Certified-Generative-AI-Developer-Professional-6x75.zip`.

Automated checks cover structure, exact blueprint allocation, per-skill plan, multiple-response spread, item-type mix, source parity, explanation pairing, CSV bytes, cross-exam duplicate wording, a token-overlap near-duplicate gate at 0.72 across all 450 stems, the absence of case wording, the absence of console-recipe phrasing, and archive reproducibility. They do not establish independent technical accuracy, psychometric quality, platform import success, or a passing result.

Practice percentages are not scaled scores, and passing the real exam requires a scaled 750 that this course cannot predict.
