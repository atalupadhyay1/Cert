## ❓ Frequently asked questions

**Is this the real exam?**
No. These are original practice questions written for this course. Nothing is copied from or recalled from a live exam, and no question bank was used as a source.

**Which version of the exam does this target?**
AIP-C01, the AWS Certified Generative AI Developer - Professional exam. The domains, tasks and skills in every explanation come from the AIP-C01 exam guide.

**How many questions are on the real exam?**
The published form is **75 questions in 180 minutes**, of which **65 are scored and 10 are unscored**; AWS does not identify the unscored items. Each set here is 75 items.

**What is the passing score?**
AWS publishes a **minimum passing score of 750 on a scaled range of 100–1000**, with compensatory scoring, so there is no separate pass mark per domain. Because 750 is scaled rather than a raw percentage, this course does not convert practice results into an expected exam score.

**Why are there no case studies?**
Because the published AIP-C01 format has none. The exam is multiple choice and multiple response only, so no case-study scaffolding is included or imitated.

**How many multiple-response items are on the real exam?**
AWS does not publish a split between multiple choice and multiple response. Each set here carries **60 multiple-choice and 15 multiple-response** items; that mix is an authoring choice and is labelled as one everywhere it appears.

**Does this cover model training?**
No, and deliberately so. The guide places model development and training, advanced ML techniques, and data and feature engineering out of scope. Items test integration, retrieval, safety, cost, and evaluation decisions instead.

**Do I need hands-on experience?**
AWS recommends two or more years building production applications plus one year of hands-on generative AI work. No prerequisite is enforced.

**How should I use the six exams?**
Take Exam 1 untimed for a baseline, study the domains where you lost most items, work through Exams 2–5 with a light clock, and finish with Exam 6 under the full 180 minutes.

**Does a good score guarantee I will pass?**
No. Practice performance is not a scaled score, and no practice result predicts the outcome of a live appointment.

**Practice percentages are not scaled scores.** A score of 80% here means you answered 60 of 75 practice items correctly; it is not an estimate of your exam result.
