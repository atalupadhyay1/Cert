## ❓ Frequently asked questions

**Is this the real PCEP-30-02 exam?**
No. All 360 questions are original educational items authored for this course and verified on CPython 3.11. Nothing is copied or recalled from a live OpenEDG exam.

**Which exam version does this target, and what about PCEP-30-03?**
This course targets **PCEP-30-02** (active). The OpenEDG Python Institute lists **PCEP-30-03** as scheduled for release on **October 31, 2026**, and states that this routine update does not affect the five-year validity of PCEP credentials already earned.

**Why are there 60 questions per practice test when the real exam has 30?**
Thirty items is a small sample across four syllabus blocks. Doubling each practice test to **60 items** lets every exam test all 41 syllabus skills in exact **18 / 29 / 25 / 28** proportions, while doubling the clock from 40 minutes to **80 minutes** preserves the exact live pace of ~80 seconds per item.

**How are interactive gap-fill and drag-and-drop items handled?**
Udemy's player supports `multiple-choice` and `multi-select`. Each exam includes at least **15 single-blank `____` code-fill / gap-fill items** formatted as multiple-choice, plus 15 five-option multiple-response items. Interactive drag-and-drop, sort, and multi-slot code-insertion widgets cannot exist in a Udemy CSV and are excluded.

**What is the passing score?**
The Python Institute requires a **cumulative average score of at least 70%** across all exam blocks. There is no separate per-block pass mark; on the live exam each item carries up to 40 points normalized to a percentage.

**Are classes, `pip`, or `open()` file streams tested here?**
No. Object-oriented programming, `pip`, file processing, closures, and decorators belong to **PCAP-31-03**, not PCEP-30-02, and are deliberately excluded.

**Why are there no case studies?**
Because PCEP-30-02 has no case-study component.
