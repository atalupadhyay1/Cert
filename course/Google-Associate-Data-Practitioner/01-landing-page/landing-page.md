## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Why 100 questions when the exam has 50–60?**
Because 100 items let each of the 42 study areas carry enough questions to be diagnosed, and 600 items let the blueprint be drilled twice without repeating a question. Every exam is labelled an expanded bank on its README, and no item count is attributed to Google.

**Q: Why are there no case studies?**
Because the Associate Data Practitioner exam does not have a case-study component. Google's certification page and the exam guide describe 50–60 multiple-choice and multiple-select questions and nothing else. A case-study block here would imitate a different certification format, not this exam, so it is omitted — and the omission is stated rather than quietly filled.

**Q: What score do I need?**
Google does not publish a passing score for ADP, so this course does not state one. Score each item correct or incorrect, group misses by local skill ID, and treat improvement per skill as the signal.

**Q: Why do some questions show configuration rather than theory?**
Because ADP asks a practitioner to read a design and decide what to change. Items show SQL, gcloud and bq commands, lifecycle rules, IAM bindings, dataset options, Dataflow windows, Composer DAG steps, Dataform configuration, and KMS key rings. Every one still tests judgement: what a change makes possible, what it prevents, and what it costs to run.

**Q: Is a Google Cloud account required?**
No. Every question is answerable from the stem and the options. No item requires you to move data or create a bucket.

**Q: How current is the content?**
The item bank follows the exam guide **v1.0** and the certification page as retrieved in October 2026. The certification page warns that the exam will soon reflect recent branding changes, so the product-name check in the publication checklist is mandatory before release, and you should confirm the guide yourself before your appointment.

**Q: Can I use this for the renewal exam?**
Not directly. Recertification is a separate, shorter instrument with its own scope. This bank is built for the standard Associate Data Practitioner exam.
