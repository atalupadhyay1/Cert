# Business-case calculations: illustrative figures, not provider prices
4.1|pay3,pay2,pay4,pay6|A pilot costs $24,000 upfront and produces $8,000 net benefit each month. Ignoring discounting, what is its simple payback period?|Divide upfront investment by constant monthly net benefit: $24,000 / $8,000 = 3 months.
4.1|roi25,roi20,roi125,roi80|A project delivers $150,000 in benefits against $120,000 total cost over the same year. Using net benefit divided by cost, what is ROI?|Net benefit is $30,000, so $30,000 / $120,000 = 25%.
4.1|time25,time33,time75,time3|Average handling time falls from 12 minutes to 9 minutes. Relative to the original duration, what is the percentage reduction?|The saving is 3 minutes from a 12-minute baseline, giving 3 / 12 = 25%.
4.1|total900,total500,total750,total650|A pilot incurs $500 in model usage, $250 in review, and $150 in monitoring each month. What is the listed monthly recurring total?|Include all three specified categories once: $500 + $250 + $150 = $900.
4.1|accepta,acceptb,accepttie,acceptnone|Model A costs $0.03 per attempt with 90% acceptance; B costs $0.02 with 50% acceptance. Ignoring other costs, which has lower cost per accepted answer?|A costs about $0.033 per accepted answer, compared with B at $0.04.
4.1|net14,net20,net26,net6|Verified monthly benefit is $20,000 and recurring monthly operating cost is $6,000. Before other unspecified costs, what is monthly net benefit?|Subtract recurring cost from the stated benefit: $20,000 - $6,000 = $14,000.
4.1|capacity100,capacity6000,capacity20,capacity5|A team saves 5 minutes on each of 1,200 tasks monthly. How much monthly capacity is released, without assuming cash savings?|The saving is 6,000 minutes, which converts to 100 hours.
4.1|pct60,pct15,pct40,pct100|A pilot attempts 1,000 tasks and completes 600 successfully. What is its successful completion rate per attempted task?|Use attempted tasks as the denominator: 600 / 1,000 = 60%.
4.1|costdouble,costless,costsame,costtriple|Request volume triples while unit variable cost falls to two thirds of its old value. Holding workload mix constant, what happens to total variable cost?|Multiplying the changes gives 3 × 2/3 = 2 times baseline cost.
4.1|budget80,budget200,budget4000,budget8000|A monthly variable budget is $4,000 and each request costs $0.05. Ignoring fixed costs, how many requests does this fund?|Request capacity is budget divided by unit price: $4,000 / $0.05 = 80,000.
