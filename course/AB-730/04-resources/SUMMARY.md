# AB-730 package summary

This course covers **Microsoft Certified: AI Business Professional (AB-730)**, the exam for business users of Microsoft 365 Copilot, Copilot Chat, Copilot Cowork, and prebuilt agents.

- Six mock exams × 100 original questions = **600**.
- Every exam is blueprint-weighted **28 / 24 / 24 / 24** percent, inside all four published ranges (25–30 / 20–25 / 20–25 / 20–25).
- Item mix per exam: **76 multiple-choice, 16 multi-select**, plus **4 case studies of 3 questions**. The twelve case items are drawn from the multiple-choice slots; eight of the multiple-choice items are sequencing items with four candidate sequences.
- The exported files therefore contain **84 multiple-choice and 16 multi-select** rows per exam, because the bulk importer accepts only those two labels.
- Multi-select stems state the required count; sequencing items offer four candidate orders and export as `multiple-choice`.
- Four official domains, 38 local study-map skills, all mapped in exports.
- Per-option explanations plus a case rationale, study-guide anchor, local skill, and primary reference on every item.
- Live exam baseline: 45-minute proctored assessment, 700/1000 scaled pass, $99 USD list with regional variation, no published item count. Study guide measured as of October 20, 2026.
- Each exam is 100 items, deliberately larger than a 45-minute assessment can plausibly be, so the blueprint can be drilled twice; it is an expanded bank, not a length replica.
- Not reproduced: hot-area items, drag-and-drop UI, labs and live tenant components, adaptive problem-solution sets, and the exact live item count.
- Offline ZIP: `AB-730-AI-Business-Professional-6x100.zip`.

Automated checks cover structure, blueprint allocation, item-type mix, source parity, explanation pairing, CSV bytes, cross-exam duplicate wording, case-study block shape, and archive reproducibility. They do not establish independent technical accuracy, psychometric quality, platform import success, or a passing result.

A practice percentage is not a scaled exam score. Microsoft does not publish a raw percentage equivalent to 700/1000.
