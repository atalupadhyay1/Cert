## Item-type fidelity boundary

## Editorial standards used while authoring

- Every item states one deciding constraint and one defensible best answer.
- Distractors are real capabilities or real governance concepts that solve a different problem, not nonsense.
- Multi-select items name the required count so the learner cannot under- or over-select by accident.
- Sequencing items anchor on a step that cannot move: permissions before enablement, classification before processing, plan before action, review before sign-off.
- Case-study items restate their specific constraint so they still read correctly outside the shared preamble.
- Terminology follows current Microsoft naming (Microsoft 365 Copilot, Copilot Chat, Copilot Cowork, Copilot Pages, Copilot Notebooks, Work IQ, agents), with capability described rather than priced.
- No pricing figure, licence entitlement, or quota is asserted as permanent; the items test the reasoning behind the choice, and the notes above tell you what to re-verify.
- No Microsoft sample, sandbox, or recall-derived item text is used. The sandbox link was consulted only to understand item formats.
- Every item is business-user level: no code, no SDK call, no CLI command, no lab step.
