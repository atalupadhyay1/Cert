# AB-730 case study briefs

Every mock exam contains four case studies of three questions. The scenario below is the shared context;
the three items that follow it are scored individually. Paste the relevant section into a Udemy lecture,
a downloadable resource, or the practice-test description when you want the scenario available before the questions.

## Exam 1

### Case study — Brightline Retail

Brightline Retail runs 210 stores and an online shop, and has just bought Microsoft 365 Copilot for 4,000 head-office and store-support staff. The merchandising team keeps 1,900 planograms and supplier agreements in SharePoint, and site permissions have not been reviewed since a reorganization two years ago. Category managers want a weekly summary of sales exceptions, while the legal team is worried that salary letters sit in the same document library as product guides. The retail director wants the tool live before the peak season, which is six weeks away, and has asked for a plan that does not create a privacy incident.

Questions in this block

1. Before Copilot is enabled for store-support staff, what must the team do about the library holding salary letters? _(D)_
2. A generated planogram summary states a supplier term that the category manager cannot find in the agreement. What should she do? _(B)_
3. Legal asks how Copilot will be stopped from showing salary letters to store-support staff. Which control answers them? _(C)_

### Case study — Cedar Health

Cedar Health is a hospital group with 11,000 clinical and administrative staff and a Microsoft 365 tenant shared with two partner clinics. The workforce team wants to answer HR questions from policy documents while individual salary letters remain restricted to the people team. Clinicians have started pasting patient summaries into Copilot prompts to shorten discharge letters, and the medical director has asked what should happen first. The group also plans to enable Copilot on clinical SharePoint sites next month, and an access review has not been run for three years.

Questions in this block

1. The workforce team wants HR answers grounded in policy documents while salary letters stay restricted. What should the approach rely on? _(C)_
2. A generated discharge letter must meet the clinical standard before a clinician signs it. What should the review check? _(B)_
3. Three teams are editing the same discharge letter template and the versions have diverged. What should they use? _(A)_

### Case study — Harborview Claims

Harborview Claims is a regional insurer with 900 claims staff and a policy library of 12,000 documents that is revised every quarter. Adjusters use Copilot to draft decision letters, and last month one letter quoted a clause that had been withdrawn, which the compliance team found during a sample review. The claims director wants every generated letter checked before it is sent, and wants to know how to make that check fast enough for 3,000 letters a day. A supplier has also uploaded a contract containing text that told the assistant to ignore its review rules.

Questions in this block

1. Adjusters draft decision letters to customers, but the letters keep reading like internal case notes. Which prompt element is missing? _(B)_
2. A generated decision letter quotes a clause that was withdrawn last quarter. What should she do? _(D)_
3. Every adjuster rebuilds the same policy context from a different set of documents each morning. What should the team do? _(D)_

### Case study — Meridian Bank

Meridian Bank has 18,000 staff, a procurement function handling 7,000 supplier contracts a year, and a contact centre that answers 40,000 customer emails a month. The bank wants Copilot to draft replies and summarize supplier risk, but group risk has refused to approve anything that could send customer data outside the tenant. A pilot team has already built a custom agent on an unmanaged site, and the security team found it could answer from documents that ordinary staff cannot open. The chief operating officer wants a single decision on what to enable this quarter and what has to wait.

Questions in this block

1. A pilot team built a custom agent on an unmanaged site. What governance step is missing? _(A)_
2. Security found the agent could answer from documents ordinary staff cannot open. What did it fail to enforce? _(D)_
3. Compliance wants evidence of what the agent did in a pilot run. What should be reviewed? _(B)_

## Exam 2

### Case study — Bluepeak Accounting

Bluepeak Accounting prepares audit files and management accounts for 1,200 small and mid-sized clients. The audit team works across Word, Excel, and PowerPoint, and the same figures are restated in all three, which is where last year's review found most errors. Partners want a pre-meeting brief before each client review and a reliable set of actions afterwards, and the operations director wants the weekly pipeline summary produced without anyone assembling it by hand. The firm is also considering a custom agent for client onboarding, but no one has checked whether the standard Copilot experience already covers it.

Questions in this block

1. The same figures are restated in a Word file, an Excel model, and a PowerPoint deck. What should the team use? _(A)_
2. A generated management commentary states a margin that differs from the model. What must happen first? _(D)_
3. Partners exchange three versions of the same client pack by email. What should they use? _(C)_

### Case study — Halden Design

Halden Design is a 90-person brand and product studio that produces campaign concepts, imagery, and client presentations. Designers generate images and mood boards with Copilot, and the studio has been asked by two clients to prove that generated assets do not breach usage rights or brand rules. The studio works with freelancers who need access to project Pages but not to the client contract folder, and the studio manager wants collaboration to happen in one live document rather than in email attachments. A new business team also wants a repeatable way to turn a discovery call into a creative brief.

Questions in this block

1. The studio wants a client-onboarding agent but no one has checked what already exists. What should it do first? _(B)_
2. Freelancers must reach project Pages but not the client contract folder. Which control applies? _(B)_
3. A generated image set is about to go to a client. What should be reviewed before approval? _(D)_

### Case study — Larkspur Consulting

Larkspur Consulting is a 600-person advisory firm whose consultants work on four or five client engagements at once. Each engagement has its own SharePoint site, a set of interview notes, and a deliverable deadline, and consultants rebuild the same context in chat every morning. The firm has signed three client contracts that prohibit storing client content in any tool outside the client tenant, and the partners want the rule enforced in practice rather than in a policy document. A junior consultant has been reusing one long chat for every client, and the quality lead cannot tell which source produced which recommendation.

Questions in this block

1. A client contract prohibits storing content in any tool outside the client tenant. What must the engagement confirm? _(D)_
2. A junior consultant has used one long chat for four different clients. What should be done with it? _(D)_
3. A consultant wants to paste raw client data into a prompt for a faster answer. What should happen first? _(A)_

### Case study — Wexford Legal

Wexford Legal is a 300-lawyer firm that drafts engagement letters, reviews supplier contracts, and prepares client briefings from large document sets. The firm has 40,000 precedent documents in an on-premises system that Copilot cannot currently see, and partners want drafts grounded in those precedents rather than in general legal knowledge. A client has asked for a cited review of case law, and the managing partner wants every citation opened by a lawyer before it reaches the client. The firm also wants to stop each team inventing its own prompt wording for the same standard letter.

Questions in this block

1. Engagement letters keep arriving as long prose when partners need a one-page summary with three fixed headings. What should the prompt specify? _(A)_
2. A draft is close but relies on a precedent the partner has rejected. What should the associate do? _(A)_

## Exam 4

### Case study — Highline Group

Highline Group is a diversified holding company with retail, logistics, and financial services divisions and 9,000 employees. Each division has its own Microsoft 365 tenant data, its own risk appetite, and its own view of what Copilot should be allowed to do, and the group CIO has to approve one rollout plan. The group has a regulatory obligation to retain certain records, a privacy programme that classifies data into four tiers, and a board that wants evidence of value before funding a wider rollout. An internal audit has found three custom agents built without an owner, and the CIO wants a standard decision path for tools, prompts, and agents.

Questions in this block

1. Divisions hold their own data and the CIO wants one rollout. What keeps a divisional user from seeing another division's content? _(C)_
2. The privacy programme classifies data into four tiers. What should the rollout use that for? _(A)_
3. Some records must be retained for years. What must be confirmed before generated content is stored? _(B)_
