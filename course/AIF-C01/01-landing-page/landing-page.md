## Course title
AWS AI Practitioner AIF-C01: 6 Tests, 600 Questions

## Course subtitle
Prepare with 6 tests, scenario-based questions, detailed answer explanations, and coverage of all 5 AIF-C01 domains.

## Course description

**Know the AI concepts—but still struggle to choose the best answer in a business scenario?**

Strengthen your preparation for the **AWS Certified AI Practitioner (AIF-C01)** certification with **6 practice tests and 600 questions**. Practice connecting AI terminology to business needs, choosing suitable AWS services, and recognizing the tradeoffs behind responsible AI decisions.

Whether you are reviewing foundation models, comparing RAG with fine-tuning, or deciding which security control a scenario requires, this course helps you move beyond remembering definitions toward explaining your choices.

### What you get

- **6 practice tests with 100 questions each:** structured practice across the five AIF-C01 domains.
- **600 original practice questions:** a mix of conceptual checks and business scenarios.
- **Single-answer and multiple-select questions:** each test contains 90 single-answer questions and 10 questions that ask you to select two answers.
- **Explanations for every option:** understand why the correct choice fits and what distinguishes the alternatives.
- **An overall rationale for every question:** reinforce the key rule or concept behind the answer.
- **AWS reference links:** continue your review with relevant AWS documentation and exam-guide material.

### Practice across all five exam domains

**1. Fundamentals of AI and ML — 20 questions per test**

Distinguish learning approaches, classification and regression, training and inference, and common data-quality problems. Practice selecting AWS services for text, speech, image, and document-processing tasks.

**2. Fundamentals of GenAI — 24 questions per test**

Review foundation models, tokens, embeddings, context windows, multimodal capabilities, and hallucinations. Explore model selection, managed AWS capabilities, and concepts such as agents, tools, memory, and orchestration across the question bank.

**3. Applications of Foundation Models — 28 questions per test**

Compare prompting, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and model adaptation. Practice choosing retrieval strategies, evaluating generated responses, and balancing quality, latency, and cost.

**4. Guidelines for Responsible AI — 14 questions per test**

Recognize fairness, explainability, transparency, privacy, robustness, and human oversight requirements. Consider how data representation, evaluation, and documented limitations affect responsible deployment.

**5. Security, Compliance, and Governance for AI Solutions — 14 questions per test**

Review permissions, encryption, private connectivity, auditing, and monitoring. Identify prompt-injection risks and appropriate practices for data access, residency, retention, and governance.

### Turn each attempt into a learning session

Start with one test to identify your weak areas. Review explanations for both incorrect answers and correct answers you guessed. Follow the AWS references, write down the distinction you missed, and apply that reasoning in your next attempt.

The sets revisit important concepts through different scenarios and wording, helping you reinforce understanding rather than rely on memorized answer letters. Selected topics vary across the sets; not every service or example appears in every test.

Allow approximately **140 minutes per 100-question test** if you want a timed practice session. This is a suggested duration for these expanded tests—not the official exam duration.

### Is this course right for you?

This course is designed for AIF-C01 candidates who have begun studying and want structured practice. It is also suitable for business analysts, product owners, managers, and cloud practitioners who want to check their foundational understanding of AI on AWS.

You do not need coding experience or a paid AWS account to answer the questions. Familiarity with basic cloud and introductory AI concepts will help you get more from the explanations.

**Build your confidence by understanding not only which answer fits, but why it fits. Use your results to focus your next study session.**

### Important course information

This is an independent practice-test course, not a video lecture series or hands-on lab course. The questions are original educational material, not actual or recalled AWS exam questions. Core concepts recur across tests; these are learning sets, not six independent predictors of exam performance.

The tests use single-answer and multiple-select formats. They do not reproduce the official exam's native interactive ordering or matching questions, exact length, or scaled scoring. Practice scores do not guarantee certification success. Use the course alongside the current official AWS exam guide and documentation.
